What is counted

Murder and non-negligent manslaughter, as defined by the FBI Uniform Crime Reporting program, per 100,000 residents. Rates use the population figure reported alongside each count.

Notes

1 1960–1967: the NYPD's own crime reporting in this period is documented as unreliable (Chronopoulos, Journal of Urban History, 2015). The figures likely undercount crime, which makes the early baseline look safer than it was. Homicide is the category least affected, which is why it is shown here.

2 2021: the NYPD submitted no data to the FBI during the switch to the National Incident-Based Reporting System. The 2021 figure (488) is the NYPD's year-end count.

3 US 2023–2024: FBI national estimates taken from a secondary copy of the Crime Data Explorer file. Provisional until checked against the FBI release.

Sources

New York City, 1960–2024: FBI UCR Return A as reported by the NYPD, via Jacob Kaplan's concatenated UCR files (openICPSR 100707).

United States, 1960–1978: FBI Uniform Crime Reports, published national estimates. 1979–2024: FBI Crime Data Explorer national estimates.
